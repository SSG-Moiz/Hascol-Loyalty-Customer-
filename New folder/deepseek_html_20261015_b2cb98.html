Delete Your Account

Request deletion of your account and associated data for the app P2P Track by P2P – Track.

How to request account deletion

  1. Fill in the form below with your name, phone number, registered email and reference number.
  2. Click Submit Request. Your request is sent to our support team at info@p2ptrack.com.
  3. We will verify your identity using the details provided.
  4. Your account and data will be deleted within 7 days of verification, and we will confirm by email.

Request form

Data that will be deleted

  • Profile information (name, email, phone number)
  • Content and files uploaded by you
  • App activity and preferences

Data that may be kept

  • Transaction or billing records – kept for up to 90 days for legal and accounting purposes
  • Backup copies – removed within 30 days of deletion
  • Anonymous/aggregated analytics data that cannot identify you

Contact

For any questions, contact info@p2ptrack.com.
